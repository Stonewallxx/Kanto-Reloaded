# Kanto Reloaded

What's new in each version, newest first. The What's New screen (on the Load Screen) shows it.

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-19)
- Kanto Reloaded, rebuilt for KIF: Every feature can be switched on or off, and its settings live in KIF Settings
- Reloaded Settings: KIF Settings, improved, with dropdowns for every choice
- Reloaded Controls: Rebind every action on keyboard and controller, with layouts
- Window Frames and Theme Mode for the game's windows and Kanto Reloaded's screens; the Pause Menu and Overworld Menu follow Theme Mode too (Dark, Glass, Light, Frost)
- Autosave settings in KIF Settings: When healing, catching, after trainer battles, every few steps
- Data Patches: Mods can add or change items, moves, Pokémon, trainers and more, and the Patch Viewer shows what each changes
- Save Manager: Notes and color tags for your saves, their health, every backup to restore, and the saves you've deleted
- The Load Screen, rebuilt: Your save's note, health and backups, and What's New
- Safe saving: A save is written in full and checked before it replaces the old one
- Bug Reports: One file with everything needed to fix a bug, a note and a screenshot, to send in Kanto Reloaded's Discord. After a crash, the game offers one before it closes and says which mod it came from
- Reloaded Pause Menu: Money, where you are and the time beside it, a row of your own, a Favorite one button away (from the map too), and every entry in one carousel
- Overworld Menu: A quick menu while walking, with 7 Quick Items, quick toggles (the Bicycle too), any setting from KIF Settings (sliders too), your quest, the Pokédex, badges, shiny odds, encounters, the Itemfinder, your party and where you are, and pages of your own
- The title screen shows PIF's, KIF's and Kanto Reloaded's versions in the top right corner
- KIF's update checks over the Load Screen in Kanto Reloaded's look: A toast at the top while they run (Everything's Up to Date when there's nothing new), a new KIF's choices with its versions, and your mods' updates with Update All
- Party Screen: Cards and details in a new look, the menu in the panel (other mods' commands too), Switch with a preview, X for the Summary and Y to move, in battle too, Dark or Light
- PokeVial: In place of KIF's Heal Pokémon, a few charges to heal your party anywhere, refilled at Pokémon Centers. A Heal Preview shows what each Pokémon gets, more charges come with Badges, and it glows when your party is badly hurt
- Speed-Up: Hold or toggle the game's speed, in its own Speed-Up section of Controls with its buttons. Burst Speed while held, the overworld and battles at their own speeds, the speed kept for next time, Shiny Slowdown, Messages at 1x, the speed in the window title and a small indicator in the corner when it changes
- TM Vault: In place of KIF's Tutor.net, every TM, HM and tutor move you've had, taught again for free, with Search, Favorites, a Type Filter, who in your party can learn each and the types it adds (Coverage). Relearn Moves opens on its Relearn tab, Move Compare shows what you'd lose, and the Checklist lists the TMs you're missing, each with where the game gives it (on the ground, in a store or as a gift)
- Reloaded Bag: A new Bag with real pocket sorting (Off, your own Custom Lists, A-Z or Number, each pocket), Search across every pocket, NEW, Favorites, views, and who in your party can use each TM or Evolution Stone. Each action explained, Toss that asks with Keep first, Add to Quick Items, Move by hand, Teach through the TM Vault, a banner saying what a picker is for, a Custom List editor with headings, starting lists with sections, and lists to export and import (old Kanto Reloaded's too). Use Several at Once for Rare Candies, vitamins, Wings, EV Berries and HP items like Potions, who in your party needs a healing item, and HELD on items your party is holding. Fixes for PIF's Bag: Sort Bag no longer flips direction, and tossing on Pinkan Island no longer redraws the berries
- Healing Moves: About 25 healing moves work outside battle with real PP, from the Overworld Menu's Heal with Moves or the party menu, with a preview before each heal and Heal Everyone to plan the most HP for the fewest PP
- Quality of Life: Toggles for an easier or quicker game (Infinite Repel, Auto-Repel Reuse, Infinite Money, Encounter Rate, Faster Evolutions and more), the ones that change how hard it is kept by each save, and the Nature Selector, Level Lock Manager and Super Candy
- Tidy folders: Kanto Reloaded's settings, logs and bug reports live in its own folder (Settings, Logging, Bug Reports), and only save things stay beside your saves. Old files move over on the first start
- Save Checker: A full check of any save or backup, on a copy. Pokémon and items the game hasn't got are kept aside until they're back, each mod's data is listed, and a save stuck on a removed mod can be made to load again
