# Reloaded Summary

Kanto Reloaded's Summary: every page on Kanto Reloaded's plates, in battle too. *Reloaded Summary* in *KIF Settings* (Kanto Reloaded's Systems) is On; Off shows the game's own Summary, with KIF's IVs and EVs.

## The Pages

Every page has your Pokémon's plate on the left, lit in its first type's colour: its name, level, gender and shiny star, types and picture, then Additional (its Hidden Power, Friendship, and Nature with the stats it raises and lowers). Its marks show in the plate's corner.

- **Info:** Its Dex number (or, for a fusion, the two it's fused from), its species, OT, ID number and held item; its Exp. with a bar to the next level; and a Memo of its nature, where and when you met it (or where it hatched) and its characteristic.
- **Stats:** Each stat with its IV, EV and base stat, and their totals. The stat its nature raises is red, the one it lowers blue; a perfect IV is gold. Under them its ability (and its Hidden Ability, when it doesn't have it), and what it's weak to and resists, with its ability counted: Water Absorb takes Water to 0, Levitate takes Ground to 0, Thick Fat halves Fire and Ice.
- **Moves:** Each move's type and PP, and the picked one's type, category, power, accuracy and description. Hidden Power shows its real type.
- **Pokédex:** Right from Moves opens its Pokédex entry, and you come back to Moves.

Along the bottom of Info and Stats: its ability's description and its Hidden Power.

## Buttons

- **Left and Right:** The pages.
- **Up and Down:** Another Pokémon (an Egg only on Info). On Moves they pick a move.
- **A:** The Options: Give Item, Take Item, Hat, View Pokédex and Mark. On Moves, A picks a move and A on another swaps the two.
- **X:** Its cry. On Stats, a fusion with two abilities opens the Abilities Card.
- **B:** Back (or stops a swap).

The mouse works too: click a tab, a move or a hint; a right click goes back.

## Learning a Move

When a Pokémon with four moves learns another, its Moves page shows the new move fifth, picked first, so you see it before choosing. A on one of its moves forgets it; A on the new one (or B) keeps its moves.

## Eggs

An Egg has its own page: how close it is to hatching, and where and when it came to you.

## Mark

Mark shows the Reloaded PC's six shapes in a row: Left and Right choose, A marks or unmarks, B is done.

## KIF's EV Points

With KIF's EV points, Stats says how many it has to spend, and A spends them there: Up and Down choose a stat, Left takes and Right gives, X puts them all back, A is done.

## Another Trainer's Team

A team that isn't yours (a rematch's View Team, a partner's Pokémon) shows view-only: no Options, swapping or changing.

## The Contest Lobby

In the contest lobby the game's own Summary shows, with its contest pages.

## Other Mods

- **Double Abilities:** A fusion's two abilities show on Stats with their HEAD and BODY badges.
- **Alpha Encounters:** An Alpha has an ALPHA badge by its name.
- **Ghostly Possession:** A possessed Pokémon shows possessed: the fusion's picture and Ghost-first types, the Ghost small in the picture's corner, the Ghost's name in Additional, and what possession changes in ghost purple (its name, raised stats, the Ghost's ability and the Ghost's move).
- **KIF: Elite Redux:** With its Innates On, an Abilities page comes between Stats and Moves: its ability and innates, each one's text, and A changes them as KIF: Elite Redux does.
