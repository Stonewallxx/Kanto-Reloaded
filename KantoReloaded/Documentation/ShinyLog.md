# Shiny Log

A record of every shiny you meet, caught or not. It's always on.

## Where It Is

- **Trainer Card:** On the Stats side, Shiny Log is first in Actions (A). A click on Shinies Found opens it too.
- **Overworld Menu:** A Shiny Log row you can put on your pages, with your shiny count in gold.
- **A Shiny's Menu:** In the Party or the PC's Actions, Shiny Log opens straight to that shiny's page.

## What It Keeps

Every shiny you meet gets an entry: wild ones (caught, or the ones that got away or were knocked out), Eggs that hatch shiny, shiny gifts and trades, and Pokémon the PC's Gamble makes shiny. Opposing trainers' shinies aren't kept.

- **The List:** Newest first (X shows the oldest first). Each row has the shiny in its own colours, its name, and the date and place. A Poké Ball marks the ones that joined you; GOT AWAY or KNOCKED OUT marks the rest.
- **The Page:** The shiny's picture in its own colours, even after it's gone; how it ended (Caught, Got Away, Knocked Out, Hatched, Received, Gambled); its types and level; when you found it, where, and how (in the grass, surfing, fishing with a rod, Wild Link with its chain, the Poké Radar with its chain, the Safari Zone, an Egg, a gift, a trade, the Gamble).
- **Took:** For a wild shiny, how many wild Pokémon you met since your last shiny, any species.
- **Your Shinies:** Under the page: your total, your longest hunt and your first find.

## Shinies You Already Have

The first time the Log sees your save, every shiny you own gets an entry from what the Pokémon remembers: where and when it was met or hatched, how, and its level then. How many encounters it took isn't known, so Took says Unknown.

Shinies the Trainer Card counted that you no longer have show as one Earlier Shinies line, so the Log's total and the Trainer Card's Shinies Found always agree.

## Playing Together

Each player keeps their own Log. A shiny you meet in a shared battle goes in both, and its page says who caught it ("ASH CAUGHT IT") and who you were with.
